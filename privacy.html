Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Exam Sathi, an educational application developed by Angad Labs. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Exam Sathi is designed to be highly secure and primarily operates offline. We may collect basic profile information (such as your Name, Father's Name, and Profile Picture) that you voluntarily enter into the app. This data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information entered in the app is used solely to generate personalized exam result cards and to enhance your local app experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

Exam Sathi may use third-party services (such as Adsterra) to display advertisements. These third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve non-personalized ads. We do not share your personal profile data with these ad networks.

4. Data Security & Deletion

Since your profile data and exam history are stored locally on your device's storage, you have full control over it. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or using the "Delete History" option within the app.
