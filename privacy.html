Privacy Policy

Exam Sathi by Angad Labs

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Exam Sathi, a premium educational platform developed by Angad Labs. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure learning environment. This policy outlines how we handle your information.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Exam Sathi is built with a privacy-first approach and primarily operates locally on your device. Any profile information you provide (such as your Name, Father's Name, or Profile Picture) is stored strictly on your local device storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store this personal data on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The local data entered in the app is used exclusively to generate customized exam result cards and to personalize your offline application experience. Angad Labs does not sell, rent, or share any personal information with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

To keep the app free for students, Exam Sathi may integrate third-party advertising services (e.g., Adsterra). These services may use standard technologies (like cookies or anonymous device identifiers) to serve non-personalized advertisements. We never share your in-app profile data with these networks.

4. Data Security & User Control

Because your profile and exam history data reside entirely on your smartphone, you maintain absolute control over it. You can permanently delete this information at any time by utilizing the "Delete History" feature within the app or by clearing the app's cache/data via your device settings.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. We encourage users to review this page occasionally. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any updates.
